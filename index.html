Privacy Policy

TUNA is a single-player arcade game developed as an indie project. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data.

Data stored on your device

The game stores the following locally on your device using the operating system's standard local storage (e.g. iOS UserDefaults):

  • your best height (record);
  • highest milestone reached;
  • unlocked achievements;
  • audio / vibration / language preferences;
  • a flag indicating that the tutorial has been completed.

This data never leaves your device and is removed when you uninstall the app.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, sign-up, or login.
  • No advertising or third-party SDKs (no AdMob, Facebook, etc.).
  • No analytics, crash reporting, or telemetry.
  • No microphone, camera, contacts, location, or photo library access.
  • No use of the Advertising Identifier (IDFA) and no App Tracking Transparency prompt.

Children's privacy

TUNA is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this same URL with a new "Last updated" date.
