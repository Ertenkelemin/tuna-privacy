Privacy Policy — TUNA

Last updated: 2026-04-27

TUNA is a single-player arcade game. We do not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

The game stores your best height, sound preference, and unlocked achievements locally on your device using the operating system's standard local storage. This data never leaves your device and is removed when you uninstall the app.

TUNA does not use accounts, advertising, analytics, or any third-party services. It does not request microphone, camera, contacts, location, or photo library access.
